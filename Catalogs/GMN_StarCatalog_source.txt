The GMN Star Catalog is built from Gaia DR3, supplemented at the bright end by
the Sky2000 Master Star Catalog v5, the Yale Bright Star Catalogue (BSC5), the
Henry Draper catalogue, and SIMBAD.

Two files are distributed. GMN_StarCatalog_LM9.0.bin ships in this repository.
GMN_StarCatalog_LM12.0.bin is downloaded on demand from
https://globalmeteornetwork.org/projects/gmn_star_catalog/ when a limiting
magnitude fainter than 9 is requested.

This file is generated by Scripts/rebuild_catalog.py from the catalog headers.
Do not edit it by hand; rebuild instead.

Limiting cut
  LM9.0 keeps a star when EITHER V or G is brighter than 9, so a red star
  whose camera-band magnitude is brighter than its V is not lost. RMS serves
  every request up to that magnitude from this file, and under the previous
  V-only cut a station working near the limit held as little as a third of the
  stars actually available to it.

  LM12.0 keeps V <= 12, using G where V is missing. Applying the same rule
  there would admit essentially the whole catalog, making the cut meaningless
  and growing a 170+ MB download by 30%. Every star in LM9.0 is present in
  LM12.0.

Header metadata
  The fields above are recorded in the file header, after the column names, as
  a JSON block introduced by the marker GMNSC-META. Every reader seeks from
  byte 12 to header_size, so the block is skipped harmlessly by all of them,
  including those that predate it. Builds before September 2026 carry no block
  at all.

  catalog_version identifies the data and is incremented on every rebuild that
  changes it. content_md5 covers the uncompressed star records only, so it
  identifies the data regardless of when the file was built; compare builds by
  it rather than by a whole-file checksum, which also covers build_date.

Photometry
  B, V are Johnson. R, Ic are Cousins. Gaia G, BP and RP are as published.
  For most stars the Johnson-Cousins magnitudes are derived from Gaia G and
  BP-RP using the Gaia DR3 transformations (Riello et al. 2021, Table 5.8).
  Brighter than G = 3.5, where Gaia BP and RP saturate, the Gaia magnitudes are
  instead synthesized from ground-based BVRI using Table 5.9. Missing
  magnitudes are NaN, never 0.0, and blended sources identified by Gaia's
  colour-corrected flux excess have their colours withheld.

Astrometry
  Positions are at the Gaia J2016.0 epoch. pmra is pmra*cos(dec), in mas/yr.

Version history
  Every build released between January 2025 and September 2026 had the V and R
  columns interchanged for all Gaia-derived stars: the column labelled V held
  the Cousins R value and vice versa.

  Version 1 corrected that, along with the Johnson-to-Cousins conversion of
  Sky2000 R and I, the J2016 epoch of the supplemental entries, and the
  bright-end photometry. It was never published.

  Version 2 adds the min(V, G) limiting cut for LM9.0 described above, and is
  the first corrected build to be distributed.
